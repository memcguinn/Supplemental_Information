# Supplemental_Information
In this repository, you will find tables of the training data used in Chapters 4 and 5. The details for each data set are outlined below.

- Chapter4_TrainingData: A complete list of the varied initial conditions used in each LES experiment (i.e., wind speed,  wind stress, mixed layer depth, temperature, and net heat flux). The resulting characteristic advective timescale $\tau_t$ (s) for each site and the corresponding characteristic chemical timescale $\tau_c$ (s) are included, as is the calculated Damkhöhler number (Da). This series provided the training data for the GPR analysis.

- Chapter5_InitialConditions: A complete list of the varied initial conditions used in each LES experiment (i.e., wind speed,  wind stress, mixed layer depth, temperature, net heat flux, CO2 flux, and salinity) with alkalinity enhancement ($\mathrm{A_T}$).

- Chapter5_TrainingData: A complete list of the varied initial conditions used in each LES experiment (i.e., wind speed,  wind stress, mixed layer depth, temperature, net heat flux, and CO2 flux) with alkalinity enhancement ($\mathrm{A^*}$). The resulting change in the volume-averaged pH levels ($\Delta$ pH) and the total DIC rate of change ($Delta c_{\mathrm{DIC}}/dt$) difference to each baseline case after alkalinity injection are included. This series provided the training data for the SVM Kernel analysis.
